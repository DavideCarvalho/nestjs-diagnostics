## Node.js 24 or newer

`@dudousxd/nestjs-diagnostics` 0.10, `@dudousxd/nestjs-diagnostics-redis` 0.4 and
`@dudousxd/nestjs-diagnostics-telescope` 0.8 require **Node.js 24 or newer** (`engines.node` is
`>=24`). Releases are tested on Node 24 and 26.

Move your app to Node 24 before you upgrade. The APIs and the envelope format are unchanged.
